How this projection works: PLC Payment/ac = 85% × (Effective Reference Price − Effective Price) × your PLC Yield, where Effective Price is the higher of the MYA price above or the national loan rate. ARC-CO Payment/ac = 85% × min[(90% × Benchmark Revenue − Actual County Revenue), 12% × Benchmark Revenue], where Benchmark Revenue = ARC-CO Benchmark Price × FSA's ARC-CO Benchmark (Olympic-average) County Yield, and Actual County Revenue = Effective Price × the 2026 Final Yield you enter above (since FSA's final 2026 county yields won't be published until well after harvest). Yield sources: ARC-CO benchmark yields are taken from FSA's 2026 ARC-CO benchmark data (non-irrigated, or all-practice where FSA does not separate practices); irrigated base in counties with a separate FSA irrigated yield is not modeled. The 2026 Expected Yield column comes from RMA (crop insurance) and is shown for reference only — FSA and RMA county yields are separate figures and can differ. Reference prices, benchmark prices/yields, and loan rates reflect the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of 2025 changes to the 2018 Farm Bill formulas (90% guarantee, 12% cap, 113%/88% effective reference price escalator), currently projected forward to the 2026 crop year. Program election matters for 2026: 2025 was the only crop year FSA paid the higher of ARC-CO or PLC automatically — every other year, including 2026, FSA pays only the program actually elected for that farm and covered commodity. Set each farm's Program Election in the table above to match what was elected; the results reflect only that program's payment, with the other program's $/ac shown for reference only. This tool provides planning-stage estimates only, using assumptions you control; it is not an official FSA payment calculation, does not reflect final USDA prices or yields, and does not account for payment limitations, adjusted gross income limits, or sequestration reductions. Confirm final figures — including your actual program election — with your local FSA office once 2026 data is published.
